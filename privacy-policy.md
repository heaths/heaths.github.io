This site is a static site hosted at **heaths.dev**. In normal browsing, it does not use analytics, advertising, or other non-essential cookies.

If you choose to use the subscribe or recommend/like features powered by [Sequoia](https://sequoia.pub), Sequoia may use the essential cookies described below so the feature you requested can work. Those features implement the [standard.site](https://standard.site) ATProto lexicon.

## Essential cookies

Because these cookies are only used to provide functionality you explicitly request, they are treated as essential cookies and do not require a cookie picker on this site.

| Cookie        | Provider | Purpose                                                                                                                 | When used                                               |
| ------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sequoia_did` | Sequoia  | Tracks publication subscription status so the subscribe experience can show whether you are already subscribed.         | When you use subscribe-related functionality.           |
| `session_id`  | Sequoia  | Third-party session cookie used to remember whether you previously signed in, so Sequoia interactions can keep working. | When you use subscribe or recommend/like functionality. |

## How these cookies are used

- These cookies support only the interactive subscribe and recommend/like features provided through Sequoia.
- They are not used for advertising or cross-site marketing on this site.
- If you do not use those interactive features, these cookies are not needed for simply reading the site.

## Third-party services

The interactive subscribe and recommend/like features depend on the third-party service [Sequoia](https://sequoia.pub). If you use those features, Sequoia may process related account or session information needed to complete the action you selected.

## Your privacy choices

If you prefer not to have these cookies used, do not use the subscribe or recommend/like features. You can also clear cookies for the relevant third-party services in your browser at any time.

## GDPR information

For visitors in the EEA, UK, or other regions with similar privacy laws, the processing described above is limited to what is necessary to provide the interactive feature you request.
